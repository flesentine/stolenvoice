Built-in image_gen tool; 2026-10-31. Final reference: `workshop-rat-dance-imagegen-v2.png`. Native hand trace: `tools/trace-workshop-rats.cjs` (13 columns including tail, 11 rows).

## Pass 1 — rejected
Too detailed at the required size, inconsistent profile directions, overly prominent pink tails. Not accepted as a native sprite source.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production reference sheet for hand-tracing very tiny rat sprites in a Sierra AGI 160x168 EGA adventure. Reference image: Rosella dance poses, for choreographic intent only; do NOT draw Rosella or human clothes. Generate ONE clean 4-column by 2-row sheet containing exactly EIGHT views of the SAME charming little GREY RAT dancing upright. The rat must remain recognizably an animal: pointed snout, one small round ear in profile, black pinprick eye, plump pear-shaped body, short forepaws and small hind feet, one thin long curling tail attached to rump. No clothes. Read order: 1 front standing with forepaws slightly out, 2 left profile taking a tiny side step with bent forepaws raised, 3 back view paws out, 4 right profile stepping with bent paws raised, 5 front both forepaws raised shoulder height, 6 left profile spinning with one hind foot lifted, 7 back quarter turn spinning with tail swept aside, 8 front settled with both feet together. Match the simple court dance turn/raised arms/twirl of reference. Traceability is essential: each rat uses approximately 9x11 chunky native pixel clusters for body plus a 4-pixel tail, NOT a detailed illustration. Each native square is shown very large with exact crisp hard stair-step edges. Every pose identical scale, same head/body proportions, same aligned foot baseline inside its cell. Plentiful magenta separation between cells, nothing touching/cropped, no labels/gridlines/text. Flat SOLID #ff00ff background, never checkerboard. Only 5 flat sprite inks: light grey #aaaaaa for most solid continuous rat body, dark grey #555555 limited shadow, black #000000 only tiny eye and a few selective contour pixels, muted pink #ff5555 only one ear-center pixel and nose/paws, white #ffffff only a tiny cheek highlight. No antialiasing, no gradients, no texture, no glow, no dither, no drop shadow, no fur strokes. Solid connected bodies and limbs, clear negative space around paws/tail; do not make a spray of scattered red pixels or a generic teddy bear. Each sprite should remain readable at an actual native height of ELEVEN pixels. Large empty areas are intentional. A practical small sprite stencil, not polished high-resolution concept art.

## Pass 2 — selected for native trace validation
Create a STRICT LOW RESOLUTION sprite stencil, not an illustration. Landscape sheet 1536x1024, FOUR columns TWO rows of the same tiny cartoon grey RAT. Each cell has ONE sprite. Every visible native pixel must be one absolutely flat hard-edged 32x32 square block, no smaller marks anywhere. The rat must fit in a MAXIMUM of ELEVEN native pixel rows and TWELVE native columns INCLUDING tail. Its body occupies only 6-7 columns. This means approximately 70 large square blocks TOTAL per sprite, not hundreds. Use large blank solid pure magenta #ff00ff margins between sprites. No text, no grid, no cell borders, no scenery. Palette exactly: magenta background, #aaaaaa light grey solid rat, #555555 dark grey minimal edge/haunch, #000000 one pixel eye per visible side, #ff5555 at most 4 pixels total for ear/nose/paws. No white, no texture or shading within a block, no antialiasing, no dithering, no gradients. DO NOT use more detail than a 1984 Sierra AGI mouse sprite. The head is a tiny pointed rat head with one 2x2 pixel ear in profile; a plump body below; short CONNECTED forepaws bent in a dance; two little hind feet on same baseline; a fine grey tail drawn with only 4 connected pixels, not a large pink snake. No clothes. Each pose is the exact same scale and same rat, and must be READABLE AT ELEVEN PIXELS HIGH. Pose order left-to-right top row: FRONT neutral forepaws out; LEFT profile with snout pointing to LEFT edge, one paw raised taking side-step; BACK view both forepaws out and NO eyes or snout; RIGHT profile with snout pointing RIGHT edge, paw raised side-step. Bottom row: FRONT both forepaws raised up to shoulder height; LEFT profile mid-twirl one hind foot lifted; BACK three-quarter mid-twirl tail swung aside; FRONT feet together and forepaws resting. Think 8 simple hand-authored arrays of square color cells. If tempted to add attractive small detail, OMIT IT. The empty background and chunky simplicity are the point. Crisp discrete color squares with solid connected silhouette and clean negative space. No fuzzy edges, no smeared pixels, no realistic anatomy.

## Acceptance checks
The generated bitmap is a reference, not an automatic palette conversion. Trace by hand into solid EGA clusters; inspect actual 11-pixel-high sprites and full dance at game scale. Require connected silhouette, ear/snouted profile, a dark eye, no eyes on back view, sparse pink accents, one fine gray tail, consistent size/ground baseline, no reversed left/right painter, and exactly Rosella's existing facing/step/arm/twirl schedule. Preserve ten-second music, entrances/exits and quest state.
