# Castle reference and trace

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Reference: `castle-agi-reference-v1.png`.

The reference is a composition study, not the game bitmap. The hand trace is in `garden()` in `../index.html`: broad shapes are authored on a 320×240 coordinate plane and rasterized directly onto the game's 160×168 picture grid. Display aspect is 100:63 to give the picture wide AGI pixels with aspect correction. All trace colors are members of the standard 16-color EGA palette; no antialiasing or gradient fills are used.

Traced features: staggered keep and spires, two large faceted corner towers, diagonal right curtain, recessed arch, twin blue/gold banners, moat, diagonal drawbridge and chains, winding yellow approach, and edge woodland. Small generated details are deliberately simplified to readable low-resolution strokes. Existing characters and puzzle objects are drawn separately with depth sorting.
