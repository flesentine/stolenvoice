# Courtyard reference

Generated to make the castle gate, shed and gallery form a coherent exterior/interior route. Original generated PNG is retained as `courtyard-reference-v1.png`; the game uses its simplified EGA contour trace.

## Prompt

Create a single playable 1986 Alder Pixel adventure-game background: inside a small medieval castle service courtyard, viewed from the garden gate behind the camera. Match classic The Star-Bell 3 EGA artistry: flat standard 16-color EGA fills, black pixel outlines, deliberately stepped edges, sparse selected stone joints, convincing layered architecture. Landscape 4:3 composition to be traced to a native 160x168 picture stretched to double-wide pixels. NO characters, NO text, NO interface. Layout is critical: broad open flagstone forecourt across entire LOWER 40 percent. At upper LEFT is a modest timber-and-stone potting shed built against the curtain wall, with one OPEN dark full-height doorway centered at 18 percent width, threshold at 60 percent height. Hanging garden tools and a terracotta pot beside this door identify the shed. At upper RIGHT is the grand stone gallery entrance with one OPEN arched doorway centered at 73 percent width, threshold also at 60 percent height, blue and gold banners identifying the royal interior. Castle red-roof tower and gray battlements behind. A warm yellow paving trail enters at BOTTOM CENTER from the garden behind viewer, forks across the flagstones toward these two doors. Both doors have clear ground approaches and no furniture in front. Exterior daylight blue sky with white clouds. Rich scene design and shadows but clean broad color regions, no noise, no gradients, no antialiasing. This is an EXTERIOR transitional courtyard between garden, shed interior and gallery interior; all visible doors must feel like real buildings.

## Navigation placement

The generated shed threshold lands near author coordinates (64, 133), and the gallery threshold near (251, 133). Both open onto the forecourt. The bottom-center yellow trail returns through the garden gate. Landing positions are below their door triggers so returning outside does not immediately re-enter the building.
