Use case: stylized-concept. One reference sprite sheet for a 1986 Sierra AGI adventure. Four columns, four rows, sixteen clearly separated poses on a plain white background, no text or borders. Rosella: long golden blonde hair, white short-sleeved blouse, royal blue dress mostly submerged, peach skin. She is struggling to swim in a river, head and shoulders above water, arms paddling and small cyan splashes. Rows: facing left, facing right, facing away, facing toward viewer. Each row is four successive paddling/treading-water frames with consistent head size and waterline, hands reaching then sweeping through the water. Face above water in every cel, not sinking. Tiny authentic hand-drawn AGI graphics: each pose designed to fit about 20x20 logical pixels, wide rectangular native pixels, thin selective contours, simple closed color fills, no shading gradients, no antialiasing, no chunky black bands, no modern pixel-art gloss, no realism. Only the sixteen standard EGA colors, especially yellow blonde hair, white blouse, light red skin, blue clothing and cyan water. Maintain the same character design, size, and waterline across all frames. This is reference art for manually redrawing as native integer-coordinate line-and-fill commands, not a final raster to paste into the game.
