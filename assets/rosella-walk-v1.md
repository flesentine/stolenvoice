# Rosella walking cycles

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, using `rosella-turnaround-reference-v2.png` as the approved identity reference.

## Saved assets

- `rosella-walk-reference-v1.png`: generated 4×4 sheet; only its front and back rows are used. Its side rows contain incorrect directions and are discarded.
- `rosella-walk-side-reference-v1.png`: separately generated four-frame right-facing walking strip. The left cycle is mirrored from these frames.
- `rosella-walk-trace-v1.json`: exact indexed game pixels, source bounds and conversion metadata.
- `rosella-walk-native-v1.png`: transparent 48×176 atlas, four columns and four rows, with 8×40 cels and gutters.
- `rosella-walk-preview-v1.png`: nearest-neighbor enlargement with the game's corrected rectangular pixel aspect. Rows are front, back, left, right.

## Conversion and playback

`../tools/trace-rosella-walk.cjs` directly converts source color regions to the same standard 16-color EGA palette as the standing sprite. A common scale within each direction, yellow-hair center alignment and a shared foot baseline keep the frames positioned consistently. Low-alpha haze and isolated small fragments are discarded. Fine details are necessarily reduced at native resolution.

The indexed frames are embedded in `../index.html`. Successful movement advances the four poses. When movement stops, the current pose stays displayed without a timer or delayed idle-frame swap. Blocked movement does not advance the pose. Each direction retains the same pixel data and ground anchor throughout movement and stopping. The earlier standing reference remains archived. No runtime image loading is required.

To rebuild the walking assets, run `tools/trace-rosella-walk.cjs` with Node.js and sharp available on NODE_PATH. If rebuilding the standing sprite with `tools/trace-rosella.cjs`, run the walking converter afterward to reinstall the walking integration.

Plain white short-sleeved blouse, dark-blue waist, long flat royal blue skirt with one dark blue fold, long flat yellow hair with minimal brown shadows, salmon skin, brown shoes. Extremely coarse authentic Sierra AGI EGA pixels, no added detail or lacing. One complete transparent sprite sheet with EXACTLY 16 full-body figures arranged in a perfectly regular FOUR COLUMN by FOUR ROW GRID. Every cell equal dimensions, generous transparent gutters; no figure touches a cell boundary. Row 1: FOUR successive frames walking toward the viewer (front). Row 2: FOUR successive frames walking away from the viewer (BACK, absolutely no face or eyes). Row 3: FOUR successive frames walking toward the LEFT (nose points LEFT). Row 4: FOUR successive frames walking toward the RIGHT (nose points RIGHT). Within each row column 1 left foot forward/right arm forward, column 2 feet passing/near under body, column 3 opposite foot and arm forward, column 4 second passing pose. Make alternating foot placements, arms swing gently, skirt hem and its single dark fold shift with legs. Keep hair, face and torso mostly steady. Standing body has same slim proportions as reference; do not make wider heads or bodies. Consistent character scale, head level and ground baseline ACROSS ALL 16 cells, fixed orthographic side/front/back camera as appropriate. Tiny deliberate rectangular pixel clusters; meant to reduce to about 8 columns by 40 rows in game. Pixel art blocks larger and coarser than typical modern pixel art. ONLY standard 16-color EGA palette: #000000 #0000AA #00AA00 #00AAAA #AA0000 #AA00AA #AA5500 #AAAAAA #555555 #5555FF #55FF55 #55FFFF #FF5555 #FF55FF #FFFF55 #FFFFFF. Fully transparent background, no floor or cast shadows. No text, labels, panel lines, grids, scenery, decorative frames, gradients, highlights, antialiasing or accessories. Maintain one consistent identity and costume throughout.
